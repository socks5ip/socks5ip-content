# Proxy Pricing in China's Market 2026

## Bottom line

**China's proxy market is a price war at the low end and a purity war at the top.** Dynamic datacenter plans start around 2–6 CNY/month; static costs 2–5x more; residential static sits higher still. Most sellers offer free tests — the real question is what each price tier actually delivers.

## What each price point buys (2026 observations)

| Tier | Typical price | What you get | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry dynamic | 2–6 CNY/mo | Datacenter dynamic, small concurrency | Shared pools, lower purity |
| Mid dynamic | 6–15 CNY/mo | More concurrency, better uptime | Bandwidth caps on some plans |
| Static | 5–20 CNY/mo | Fixed exit, datacenter or residential | Confirm shared vs dedicated |
| Residential static | 10–40+ CNY/mo | Home-broadband exit, stable | The premium buys "looks like a real user" |
| Big-bandwidth (video) | 20–40 CNY/mo | 100M-class lines, video/live ready | Only worth it if you move big data |

Specific examples from mainstream Chinese providers (2026, promotional prices): a 100M dedicated static line at ~8 CNY/month, entry dynamic residential at 4 CNY/month, ISP-residential matrix lines around 10 CNY/month, and top-tier dedicated residential at ~35 CNY/month. L2TP soft-router plans commonly start around 6 CNY/month and scale with bandwidth.

## Price ≠ quality: what the cheap tier hides

- **Shared exits** carry "guilt by association" — one bad neighbor on the same IP range gets your account flagged too.
- **Purity gaps**: datacenter ranges show up on blacklists more often.
- **Fine print**: concurrency limits, rotation frequency, and bandwidth caps live in the details — ask before buying.

## When it's worth paying more

- **Account-sensitive work** (platform operation, ads verification): residential static premium is cheap insurance against a flagged account.
- **Live streaming / video work**: bandwidth tier beats stacking more cheap lines.
- **Long-horizon stable projects**: static + longer billing cycles.

## Practical rules

1. **Test first**: every legit seller offers a free test; verify region, purity and uptime before paying.
2. **Start small**: buy the smallest cycle, run real tasks, then extend for the discount.
3. **Compare**: 20+ providers' current plans are indexed at the price center (canonical link above) — most quote "X CNY/month onwards" and prices shift with promos, so confirm on the official console before ordering.
4. **Region matters more than price** for cross-border work — a wrong-region exit is worthless no matter how cheap.
